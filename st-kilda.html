Airbnb cleaning in St Kilda

Inner Turn runs short-stay changeovers in St Kilda for property managers and hosts: the clean, linen and restock between a checkout and the next check-in. The standard turn uses your linen on site. A linen-partner add-on (supply and laundry) is quoted separately per bed count and is not on the card. Inner Turn does not launder linen itself. St Kilda changeover FAQ

How much is an Airbnb changeover in St Kilda?

The live card: 1-bed $149, 2-bed $189, 3-bed $229, 4-bed $279. 5+ bedrooms or a house is quoted. Same-day adds $40–$70 by size, and published event weeks add 25%. GST not charged. Full card at innerturn.com.au/pricing.

Who does the clean at my St Kilda listing?

An insured ABN contractor on Inner Turn's FILE: ABN, current public liability certificate and written rates are checked before any job is offered. The desk itself does not clean or attend the lot. The desk holds no keys.

What do I get back after the changeover?

A photo pack on your manager thread before the next guest: timestamped stills of the entry, each bed, bathroom, kitchen bench, living area and any visible damage. A missing pack means the job is not done.

Which St Kilda lots does Inner Turn not take?

Buildings where the owners corporation bans short stays, lots where the desk would have to attend, because the desk holds no keys, and lockboxes left empty after checkout.
