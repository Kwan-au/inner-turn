                  What happens next

                  1. Kwan reads it and replies by email with a written price per turn. GST not charged, so the quoted price is the invoice.
                  2. If the price suits, send a lot pack for your first turn: suburb and street, beds, checkout and next check-in, and how the contractor gets in. Codes go on the email thread, never on a form.
                  3. You confirm in writing. Then a contractor on file accepts the window. If we can't put someone on it, we tell you and cancel our side. We never send an uninsured stand-in.
