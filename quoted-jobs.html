Off the card · quoted in writing

Bigger place or outside our suburbs? It's quoted.

The published card covers 1 to 4 bedrooms in eight inner-ring suburbs. 5+ bedrooms, houses and every other suburb get a written quote from the desk before anything is booked. It is still overflow only: you keep the guest thread, PMS and listing.

Which jobs are quoted

  • 5 or more bedrooms.
  • Houses.
  • Any suburb outside the eight on the card.

On the card: Richmond, South Yarra, Southbank, Docklands, East Melbourne, Fitzroy, Collingwood and St Kilda. Everywhere else is quoted. Check a suburb on the areas page.

How a quote works

  1. Send the details: suburb, property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, and the checkout date and time. Name, next check-in, linen and notes are optional. No street address, access details or codes on the form.
  2. The desk replies with a written quote. There is no price until it is in writing. GST is not charged, so the quoted price is the invoice.
  3. If the quote suits, send a lot pack for the first turn.
  4. You confirm in writing, and a contractor on FILE accepts the window. If no one on FILE can take it, the desk tells you and cancels its side.
  5. The photo pack lands on your job thread before the next guest.

A form submission is a lead, not a booking. A booking needs the lot pack, your written confirm, and a contractor on FILE.

What a written quote fixes

  • A no-show the next guest finds. A contractor on FILE accepts the window before any job exists, and the photo pack lands on your thread before the next guest.
  • A price that moves after booking. The quote is in writing before anything is booked. The quoted price is the invoice. GST is not charged.
  • No proof of condition. A timestamped photo pack on your thread. Missing pack = not done.
  • Keys and codes. The desk holds no keys. The form never asks for door, lockbox or gate codes.

What stays the same

  • Overflow only. You keep the guest thread, PMS and listing.
  • The desk holds no keys.
  • The desk operator does not attend the lot.
  • The standard turn uses your linen on site.
  • An owners-corporation short-stay ban means the desk walks away.

The card, for comparison

Inside the ring, up to 4 bedrooms: 1-bed $149, 2-bed $189, 3-bed $229, 4-bed $279. Same-day and event-week rules are on the live card. GST is not charged.
