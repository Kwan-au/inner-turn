A form is not a booking

Forms use a honeypot and a short timing gate so a blank machine post is dropped. That does not make a lead into a book.

How the desk is locked

Short appendix. The public site does not use these locks. They sit on the operator desk only.

The desk is invite-only on Netlify Identity. Sign-in is email + password, allow-listed to desk@innerturn.com.au and the Gmail recovery address. Public signup stays off. Desk APIs accept only a token that GoTrue / Netlify Identity has verified. Unsigned or forged JWTs are rejected.

After Identity succeeds, a second lock is required: a one-time six-digit code emailed to the desk inbox. It works once, only for the account that asked, and expires after ten minutes; the server keeps only a keyed hash of it in Netlify Blobs. A hashed unlock phrase remains as a fallback. A session token lasts twelve hours and is required, with the Identity token, before pipeline state can be read or written.

Five failed Identity tries on the page pause for a minute. Eight failed unlocks lock that address for fifteen minutes.

HTTPS only. HSTS. Clickjacking blocked. Content-Security-Policy denies unexpected scripts. Desk and login pages are no-store and noindex. Vulnerability mail: desk@innerturn.com.au — also listed in security.txt.
