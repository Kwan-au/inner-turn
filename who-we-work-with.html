Managers and cleaners. Overflow only.

Two sides of one desk. Managers keep the listing. Cleaners join FILE. Neither side is a co-host arrangement.

Short-stay managers and property managers

You keep the guest thread, the PMS and the listing. We dispatch an insured contractor on FILE. The photo pack comes back on your thread before the next guest.

This fits inner-Melbourne books, including portfolios of several lots. On the card: Richmond, South Yarra, Southbank, Docklands, East Melbourne, Fitzroy, Collingwood and St Kilda. Outside that ring, the price is a written quote. An owners-corporation short-stay ban is a walk.

The standard turn uses your linen on site. A linen-partner add-on (supply and laundry) is quoted separately per bed count and is not on the card. Inner Turn does not launder linen itself.

Cleaners

Joining FILE needs an ABN, a current public-liability certificate of currency, and a written rate. A website form is an application, not a job.

The job arrives as a private link. Do not message the guest. The photo pack is required. A missing pack means the job is not done, and pay stays on hold.

What we are not

  • Not a co-host. We do not take the guest thread or a commission on the booking.
  • Not a traditional cleaner who takes the keys. The desk does not hold keys by default, and the operator does not attend the lot.
  • Not a property manager. Listing, PMS and owner stay with you.

Trust notes

  • ABN 20 235 092 068. The operator is not registered for GST. The published card is the invoice.
  • FILE first. No contractor is offered a job until ABN, certificate and written rates are on file.
  • Keys stay with you: lockbox, app, or a named person. Never put a code on a public form.
  • A form is a lead. A booking needs the lot pack, your written confirm, and a contractor on FILE.
