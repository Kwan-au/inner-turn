Same-day and event weeks

Same-day extra only with a written yes from the contractor. A window moved after 8pm the night before is treated as same-day. Event weeks add 25% when checkout or check-in falls in a published window: Cup carnival 31 Oct–7 Nov 2026, AO 11–31 Jan 2027, F1 Albert Park 1–4 Apr 2027.

Trial

Trial −$15 once, and only if the desk still keeps its floor. The 1-bed card is not cut to match a cheaper flyer.

What the card includes

  • Dispatch of an insured contractor on FILE.
  • The turn.
  • A timestamped photo pack on your thread. The pack records the turn. It does not add cleaning tasks. Photo pack spec.

The turn is the standard changeover in the terms: strip and remake beds with the linen agreed in the lot pack; bathroom and kitchen to guest-ready; floors vacuumed and wet areas mopped; bins out; lights and climate reset; lockbox or app left as instructed.

Linen and restock only if you write them into the lot pack and the contractor accepts that scope. The standard turn uses your linen on site. A linen-partner add-on (supply and laundry) is quoted separately per bed count and is not on the card. Inner Turn does not launder linen itself. Send a lot pack.

What it does not include

Listing takeover, guest chat, holding keys, the operator on site, the VIC short-stay levy, or GST. Platform damage claims stay with you. The desk holds no keys.

How this sits in the Melbourne band

Published 1-bed clean-only rates in inner Melbourne often start near $130 plus GST. This card is $149 and GST is not charged. We do not drop the 1-bed to chase a $118 floor. Volume discounts are not published; send the lot pack if you have a book of inner-ring turns. For several lots or buildings, ask for a free listing quote.
