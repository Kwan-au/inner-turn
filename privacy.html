Privacy policy

Inner Turn is a changeover desk. We collect only what is needed to quote, book, dispatch and invoice a turn — and to keep a cleaner on file.

What we collect How we use it Who sees it How long we hold it Your rights Contact

1. What we collect

From managers and owners: name, company, email, mobile, invoice entity, suburb or address, bed count, checkout / check-in window, access method and codes, linen and restock notes, damage notes, and the content of the email or form thread.

From cleaners applying to the file: name, company, email, mobile, ABN, certificate of currency details, written 1/2/3/4-bed rates, and photo-report capability.

From the site itself: form submissions via Netlify Forms, basic server logs, a first-party page-path beacon (path and referring host only — no cookies, no advertising pixel), and the timestamp we attach to a form to reduce spam. We do not run advertising pixels on this site.

We do not want guest passport images, payment-card full numbers, or listing-platform passwords. Do not send them.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to a lot pack or a cleaner rate file
  • To dispatch a contractor and produce the photo pack
  • To invoice and, if needed, pause a book for non-payment
  • To keep the desk file (LEAD / FILE / LIVE) accurate
  • To meet a legal or insurance request if one is made

Guest personal data that happens to sit in a lot pack (a name on a lockbox note, a message forward) is used only to complete that stay. It is not reused for marketing and is not added to a list.

3. Who sees it

Access details and the job brief go to the contractor assigned to that turn — only that contractor, only for that window.

Mail and forms sit on Gmail (kwan.ajak87@gmail.com as the live outbound desk) and on the Outlook mailbox desk@innerturn.com.au. Site forms are processed by Netlify. Domain and DNS sit with GoDaddy / auDA. Desk sign-in uses Netlify Identity plus a one-time code emailed to the desk inbox (only a keyed hash of the code is kept, in Netlify Blobs, and only until it is used or expires), with a hashed unlock phrase as a fallback. We do not sell lists.

We may disclose information if required by Australian law, a court, or to establish or defend a claim about a specific job.

4. How long we hold it

Live access codes are updated when you send a change and are not kept as a public page. Job threads, invoices and photo packs are kept for at least seven years where they form part of the business record. Cleaner ABN / CoC files are kept while the contractor is on the bench and for a short period after they drop off, so a past job can still be evidenced.

This site is served over HTTPS. Do not treat email as a vault — send a new code on the thread rather than a spreadsheet of every door.

5. Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete a lead that never became a job. Write desk@innerturn.com.au. We may keep a job record that we still need for tax, insurance or a live dispute.

If you are unhappy with how we handle personal information you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
