Overflow only

Changeovers only. One clear scope.

We dispatch an insured contractor on FILE for the interval between guests. You keep the listing, PMS and guest thread. The photo pack lands on your job thread before the next guest.

What a standard changeover includes

  • Strip and remake beds with your linen.
  • Bathroom and kitchen guest-ready.
  • Floors vacuumed and wet areas mopped.
  • Bins out.
  • Lights and climate reset.
  • Lockbox or app left as instructed.
  • A timestamped photo pack on your thread. Missing pack = not done. Pay HOLD.

The standard turn uses your linen on site. A linen-partner add-on (supply and laundry) is quoted separately per bed count and is not on the card. Inner Turn does not launder linen itself. Linen and restock only if you write them into the lot pack and the contractor accepts that scope.

What it does not include

  • Listing takeover, or the guest thread.
  • Guest messaging.
  • Holding keys as the default. The desk operator does not attend the lot.
  • Deep cleans and trades: carpet steam, oven deep-clean, mould, or other trades, unless quoted in writing.
  • Platform claims. Those stay with you. The desk flags visible damage with photos. It is not a claims agent.

The published card

  • 1-bed $149 · same-day +$40
  • 2-bed $189 · same-day +$50
  • 3-bed $229 · same-day +$60
  • 4-bed $279 · same-day +$70
  • 5+ bedrooms and houses — quoted

Same-day extra only with a written yes from the contractor. A window moved after 8pm the night before is treated as same-day. Event weeks add 25% when checkout or check-in falls in a published window. GST is not charged. This number is the invoice. The full list is the live card.

On the card: Richmond, South Yarra, Southbank, Docklands, East Melbourne, Fitzroy, Collingwood and St Kilda. Outside that ring, the price is a written quote. An owners-corporation short-stay ban is a walk.

How it books

  1. Free quote. The form is a lead, not a booking. Get a free listing quote.
  2. Lot pack: suburb and street, beds, checkout and next check-in, and access that does not need the desk. No lockbox or door codes on the public form. Send a lot pack.
  3. Your written confirm, and a contractor on FILE who accepts.
  4. Photo pack on the thread before the next guest. Photo pack spec.
