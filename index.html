Desk answers

Eight questions PMs ask first.

What is a lot pack?

The written job file for one stay: suburb and street, beds, checkout and next check-in, and how the contractor gets in without the desk on site.

Is the form a booking?

No. The form creates a lead. A booking needs the lot pack, your written confirm, and a contractor on FILE.

Do you take over the listing?

No. Listing, PMS and guest chat stay with you. Do not tell the guest we are the host.

Why is GST not charged?

The operator is not registered for GST. The card is the amount invoiced. ABN 20 235 092 068.

What if the building bans short stays?

We walk. An OC short-stay ban is not a job.

When is same-day extra charged?

When the window moves after 8pm the night before, and only with a written yes from the contractor. Extras: +$40 / +$50 / +$60 / +$70.

Who handles damage claims?

Platform claims stay with the manager. The desk flags visible damage with photos. It is not a claims agent.

Can I cut the 1-bed to match a cheaper flyer?

No. The 1-bed card stays $149. Trial is −$15 once if the desk still keeps its floor.
