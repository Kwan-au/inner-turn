Desk run sheet — finish the job

Kwan stays off site. If a step needs a body in the lot, it is the contractor’s.

  1. A

    Inbox

    Outlook desk@ first. Ignore Gmail threads unless the manager still replies there.

  2. B

    Margin lock

    1-bed keep $20–$39 (pay $110–$129). 2-bed keep ≥ $30. 3-bed keep ≥ $40. Else decline.

  3. C

    Dispatch

    Text the cleaner: address, window, linen, restock, “photos before you leave.” No keys through Kwan.

  4. D

    Hold the manager

    One line: crew booked, report before check-in. Do not promise “I will be there at 11.”

  5. E

    Collect the pack

    Kitchen, baths, beds, living, balcony, damage. If pack is thin, send the cleaner back — you do not go.

  6. F

    Close

    Forward pack. Invoice the card. Pay the cleaner the written rate. Log it on /dash.
