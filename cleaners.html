How it works

  1. 1

    Send your 1/2/3-bed rates and certificate of currency using the form below.

  2. 2

    We check your ABN and public liability cover.

  • Your own transport and supplies
  • Reliability with checkout and check-in windows
  • Photos on every turn
  • A National Police Check is welcome but not required

Questions

Is this a job?

No. Going on file is not a job offer, not employment, and not a guarantee of work. It is contractor work offered turn by turn. You accept or decline each written offer.

Do I need an ABN?

Yes. An active ABN is required. We check it before offering any turn.

Do I need public liability insurance?

Yes. Current public liability insurance is required. Upload the certificate of currency with the form, or confirm you will email it. We check cover before offering any turn.

Do I need a police check?

A National Police Check is welcome but not required. Some property managers prefer it, so having one may mean more turns are offered to you.

Which suburbs are turns offered in?

Turns on the published card are in Richmond, South Yarra, Southbank, Docklands, East Melbourne, Fitzroy, Collingwood and St Kilda. See areas we offer turns in and all areas. Other suburbs are quoted. Tick every area you can cover on the form.

How are turns offered?

When a property manager confirms a turn in an area you cover, we offer it in writing with the address window, times, bedrooms and the pay for that turn. You accept or decline. There is no fee to join and no lock-in.

How much work is there?

It depends on bookings in your area. We don't promise a set number of turns. Published event weeks (Cup carnival, the Australian Open, F1 Albert Park, and the other windows on event weeks) can bring extra turns. They are still offered one at a time, in writing, and are not a promised volume.

How and when do I get paid?

Per completed turn. The amount is in your written offer before you accept, and we pay your invoice by bank transfer within 14 days of the photo pack going on the thread.

Do I need my own supplies?

Yes. You bring your own transport and supplies.

Is there a fee or a lock-in?

No fees to join, and no lock-in. You set your own availability and can say no to any turn.
