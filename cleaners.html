Independent cleaners · Melbourne

Join Inner Turn's contractor file

We pass short-stay changeover work around Melbourne to independent, insured cleaners. Going on file isn't a job offer or a guarantee of work. It means we can offer you turns when they come up.

Send your details How it works

Already on file? Open your job thread, tap Cleaner and enter the code from your offer.

How it works

  1. 1

    Send your details using the form below.

  2. 2

    We check your ABN and public liability cover.

  3. 3

    When a property manager confirms a turn in your area, we offer it to you in writing with the address window, times, bedrooms and pay for that turn.

  4. 4

    You accept or decline. If you accept, you do the turn and send the photo pack on the thread before the guest arrives.

What we ask for

  • An active ABN
  • Current public liability insurance
  • Your own transport and supplies
  • Reliability with checkout and check-in windows
  • Photos on every turn
  • A National Police Check is welcome but not required

Contractors aren't employees. You set your own availability and can say no to any turn.

What you get

  • Work offered in writing before you go
  • Clear scope for each turn
  • No lock-in
  • No fees to join
  • Paid per completed turnRates depend on the property size and scope, and are shown in each written offer.

Each turn's pay is in the written offer before you accept. Send your invoice once the photo pack is on the thread, and we pay by bank transfer within 14 days.

Questions

Is this a job?

No. It's contractor work offered turn by turn.

How much work is there?

It depends on bookings in your area. We don't promise a set number of turns.

Do I need my own supplies?

Yes.

How and when do I get paid?

Per completed turn. The amount is in your written offer, and we pay your invoice by bank transfer within 14 days of the photo pack going on the thread.

Do I need a police check?

It's not required, but some property managers prefer it, so having one may mean more turns are offered to you.
